# Nether lava transition into DH

The screenshot showed a strong color transition between real terrain lava and DH lava. Source inspection found different material handling: normal lava uses textured luminance-driven emission and recoloring; DH lava used emission 1.5 plus generic terrain noise and lighting.

This WIP candidate introduces a shared world-space distant lava color and emission (3.0). Normal lava fades into that appearance between 20% and 35% of the shader's vanilla far distance, completing before the existing DH coverage fade begins at 40%. Close lava retains its detail. Nether DH lava uses the same shared appearance, disables directional shading and vanilla AO, zeros its lightmap, and skips generic DH terrain color noise. Normal lava's extra material recolor fades out with the blend.

Scope: Nether + DH only. Existing 750-1250 block fog curve, Overworld/End material handling, fluid immersion behavior, DH distance, world data and render-distance settings are unchanged. This is a visual approximation: coarse LOD geometry and other screen-space shadow/postprocess differences can remain.
