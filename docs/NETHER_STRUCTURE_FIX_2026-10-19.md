# Nether structure clearance and foundations — WIP 0.9.0

Gameplay follow-up, 2026-10-20: the user reported "it works, good enough for now" and requested including this fix in the next big update. This confirms their WIP test of the structure fix; it does not establish that every structure/seed or the subsequently implemented submarine balance has been gameplay tested.

User report: a freshly regenerated test Nether contained submerged Golem Factory structures and bastions hanging in bare air, with no terrain island underneath. Existing user saves were not opened or edited during this fix.

## Findings

- The old height adjustment only targeted vanilla bastions and fortresses. Golem Dungeons' Piglin Factory uses an absolute starting height of 33; actual baseline bounds began at Y32 while our lava reaches Y99. LTAB's Nether house projected to a location with bounds beginning at Y99. Both were reproduced with the actual installed structure mods in disposable worlds.
- Early noise foundations alone did not satisfy all post-placement support checks on raised modded structures. The first candidate left one of nine base samples unsupported on both the factory and house. The final candidate adds a post-placement foundation pass below complete template bounds.
- Vanilla `PoolElementStructurePiece.move` translates the template origin and bounding box, but not the absolute ground Y of terrain junctions. The previous bastion shift left those junctions behind. They now move with their pieces.
- Baseline samples of all four bastion root layouts had support at the sampled whole-complex base points; the user's exact bare-air bastion was not reproduced without its seed/coordinates. The new final support pass covers bastions as well as modded complexes, and the final checks include their full sampled base area rather than assuming that an early noise pass is sufficient.

## Installed behavior

`mods/apocalypse-nether-islands-0.9.0.jar` replaces 0.8.0 in WIP only.

- The clearance pass applies to structures generated by the custom Y100 lava-sea preset with the foundations marker and actual Nether biome source. It also checks the actual level dimension when a level accessor is supplied. This accommodates vanilla passing a chunk height accessor during generation; requiring a level accessor unconditionally disabled the hook in an intermediate candidate and was rejected by the tests.
- Whole piece layouts move together so their lowest bounds are at least Y112. A high structure can move downward as a complete layout to keep its roof within the dimension. Structures too tall to satisfy both limits produce a diagnostic rather than silently clipping or being deleted; none of the tested layouts hit that condition.
- Modded complexes receive whole-envelope islands below their lowest template bounds, as bastions already did. Fortress bridge supports retain their per-piece behavior.
- A final `StructureStart.placeInChunk` pass restores missing netherrack below the complete complex after its templates run. It uses the existing rounded, tapered island shape, writes only inside the current generation chunk/clip and below all template bounds, and only replaces air/liquid. It does not fill rooms or overwrite placed solid blocks/containers. Fortress bridge layouts are excluded from this whole-envelope final pass.
- Existing apron references remain in place. Existing ruined-portal removal is preserved.

The lava level, island layers, basalt floor, debris distribution/yield and submarine configs were not changed. This patch changes future terrain/structure generation; it does not retrofit already generated floating or submerged structures.

## Verification

The dedicated native audit loads the actual Create/LTAB, Golem Dungeons, Modular Golems and L2 dependencies, the L2 serial fix and ruined-portal removal, alongside zFastNoise, BOP, TerraBlender, GlitchCore and Lithostitched, with the current WIP datapack. This is the relevant structure/worldgen stack, not a full-client gameplay/rendering test. Optional Patchouli/Twilight integration resources produce warnings in the reduced test environment; the structure-generation audit completed successfully.

The Nether generator's enabled structure sets identified exactly five eligible structure IDs:

| Structure | Coverage |
|---|---|
| `minecraft:fortress` | Natural start, height/build bounds; existing per-piece support logic preserved |
| `minecraft:bastion_remnant` | Natural starts covering units, hoglin stable, treasure and bridge layouts; post-FEATURES base checks |
| `minecraft:nether_fossil` | Natural start, height/build bounds and post-FEATURES base checks |
| `create_ltab:nether_roof_house` | Natural start, height/build bounds and post-FEATURES base checks |
| `golemdungeons:piglin_factory` | Natural start, height/build bounds and post-FEATURES base checks |

Final result: ten natural structure samples, all four bastion roots, zero structure failures. **1,338/1,338** sampled positions were solid immediately below the potential template-extension layer, and **1,338/1,338** were solid deeper inside the island. All starts met the Y112 minimum and stayed below build height. Ruined portals were absent from the enabled structure inventory. This sampling does not exhaust all seeds or prove the appearance of the user's exact previously observed bastion.

Source: `scripts/nether_islands/`. Reproduction uses `build_addon.py --tests` and `run_native_audit.py --all-structures --fastnoise --biomes` with a new output directory. Production bytecode was compared class-for-class with the passing test artifact. Production excludes the test mod. Installed jar and packwiz hashes were verified. Pack version remains v1.3.2.
